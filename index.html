Mexico City

Mexico City is the capital and largest city of Mexico, located in the Valley of Mexico in the high plateaus of central Mexico. It is bordered by the State of Mexico to the north, east, and west, and by the state of Morelos to the south. The city is a major center of finance, commerce, culture, education, and technology in Latin America, as well as a premier tourist destination known for its historic landmarks, museums, and gastronomy. Home to the headquarters of the Mexican federal government, it is the most populous city in North America and the oldest capital city in the Americas.
